youtube-ing

youtube-ing is a private tool operated by nightcabin.lab to manage its own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it does

  • Uploads videos, thumbnails and descriptions to the operator's own YouTube channels.
  • Reads the operator's own channel and video statistics, including YouTube Analytics, to produce internal performance reports.
  • Reads and posts the operator's own comments on the operator's own videos.

It accesses only channels owned by the operator, using Google sign-in that the operator approves personally. It runs on the operator's own computer; there is no public service and no server storing other people's data.

youtube-ing（中文說明）

youtube-ing 是 nightcabin.lab 自用的私人工具，用來管理自己的 YouTube 頻道。它不對外提供服務，也沒有其他使用者。

  • 把影片、縮圖與說明上傳到營運者自己的頻道。
